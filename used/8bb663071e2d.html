A Design That Balances Utility and Style

From the front, the 2026 Pacifica Select presents itself with a refined grille and modern lighting signature that feel both bold and welcoming. The body lines flow smoothly, helping to reduce visual bulk while maintaining presence. At the rear, the sleek taillight design and a smooth tailgate finish enhance the look without overreach.

Even with its ample size, the Pacifica Select remains navigable in suburban streets and tighter parking situations. Features like power sliding doors and a power liftgate help with loading and unloading in real-life settings. The design speaks to both form and function.

Interior Comfort That Holds Up

In the Pacifica Select, Chrysler ensures that comfort and utility coexist. Its roomy cabin supports full family life; multiple rows of seating, cargo flexibility, and thoughtful details at every turn. Technology That Enhances, Not Distracts

The 2026 Pacifica Select introduces improvements to infotainment and connectivity. The touchscreen interface is responsive and paired with a digital driver display to present key info clearly. Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto come standard, integrating your phone apps seamlessly.

Features like a digital key (in supported configurations) allow your smartphone to act as your entry and start tool. Remote access via a connected vehicle app lets you precondition cabin temperature, check vehicle status, or lock/unlock from your phone. Tech here is there to help your daily flow, not hinder it.

Safety for Every Passenger

The Pacifica Select includes a full suite of driver assistance and safety technologies. Forward Collision Warning with automatic emergency braking, Blind Spot Monitoring, Lane Keeping Assist, and Rear Cross Traffic Alert help monitor the surroundings and react when necessary.

Optional features may include surround-view camera systems that aid in parking and tighter maneuvers. Features like Safe Exit Warning help alert occupants if a passing vehicle is approaching before they open doors.

The body structure is engineered to manage crash energy intelligently, giving occupants protection from all rows.

Driving That Matches Its Promise

Behind the wheel, the 2026 Chrysler Pacifica Select feels composed and trustworthy. The suspension smooths out road irregularities with confidence. Steering is direct and predictable, translating driver input without fuss.

The V6 power and 9-speed transmission respond well under different loads. Even when fully loaded, the Pacifica Select holds its pace. Road noise is controlled, allowing cabin conversations and entertainment to come through clean. For many families, this minivan feels like a good partner for day in, day out.

Made to Handle Real Conditions

In British Columbia and its surroundings, drivers demand vehicles that can handle varied weather and terrain. The Pacifica Select supports that with competent build quality. Heated features, robust climate control, and visibility enhancements help with winter mornings. The potential for all-wheel drive in certain trims adds grip when roads get slippery.

This minivan isn't built for extreme off-roading, but it's well-suited to the demands of mixed driving conditions while putting comfort and usability first.
