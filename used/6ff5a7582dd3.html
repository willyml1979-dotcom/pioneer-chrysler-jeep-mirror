When Two Rows Aren't Quite Enough

The biggest distinction between the Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L is passenger capacity. Theextended Grand Cherokee L adds a third row, creating seating for up to seven people and providingfamilies with considerably more flexibility. That extra row can be valuable even if you do not use everyseat every day. It provides room when children bring friends home, relatives visit or everyone needs totravel in one vehicle.

When passengers are not the priority, the cabin can be reconfigured for cargo. Folding the second andthird rows opens up as much as 2,395 litres of cargo capacity, creating space for luggage, sportsequipment, camping supplies, home-improvement purchases or the considerable amount of stufffamilies somehow accumulate.

Laredo Covers the Important Stuff

Choosing the Laredo does not mean choosing an SUV stripped of the technology drivers use every day. A10.25-inch digital cluster display places important information directly ahead of the driver, whileUconnect 5 technology provides access to infotainment and smartphone connectivity. Apple CarPlaysupport and Android compatibility make it easier to bring familiar apps, navigation and entertainmentinto the vehicle.

The interior is designed around practical family use. The three-row configuration provides additionalpassenger flexibility, while the available seating arrangements make it easier to adapt the cabin whenthe day's priority changes from people to cargo. That versatility is one of the Grand Cherokee L'sstrongest arguments. It can handle the school run in the morning, errands in the afternoon and a fullyloaded family getaway when the weekend arrives.

Capability Still Matters

Adding a third row has not changed the expectations attached to the Jeep name. The 2027 GrandCherokee L Laredo retains standard 4x4 capability, providing useful traction when roads become morechallenging.

The Selec-Terrain Traction Management System allows the vehicle to adjust its behaviour for differentdriving conditions. That capability may sound like something reserved for off-road adventures, but it is

equally relevant when Canadian weather produces snow-covered streets, slippery rural roads or messyhighway conditions.

The Grand Cherokee L Laredo is available with Jeep's 3.6L Pentastar V6, producing up to 293horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque. The established V6 provides the kind of smooth, predictable powerdelivery that suits a vehicle designed for commuting, family transportation and longer highway trips.

Properly equipped Grand Cherokee models can also tow up to 2,812 kilograms, or 6,200 pounds. Thatcapability adds another dimension for families with a recreational trailer, boat or other equipment theywant to bring along.

A Fresh Look for 2027

The 2027 Grand Cherokee receives exterior updates that keep its appearance current withoutabandoning the familiar Jeep design language. Revised front-end details include updated fascias, grilletreatments and redesigned headlamps. Laredo models also feature a new 18-inch machined and paintedwheel design. The overall result remains recognizably Grand Cherokee: clean and upscale enough fordaily use while maintaining the stronger stance buyers expect from a Jeep SUV.

The Sensible Side of Grand Cherokee L

The Laredo makes a compelling case for buyers who care more about the fundamentals than loading anSUV with every luxury option available. You get three rows, flexible cargo space, modern cabintechnology and Jeep 4x4 capability in a vehicle designed to handle everyday family demands. It isspacious without becoming cumbersome and capable without requiring every trip to feel like anexpedition.
